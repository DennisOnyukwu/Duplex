# Canada: competitor report (2026-11-25)

All prices, follower counts and claims below were seen on 2026-11-25 at the URL given. CAD unless marked USD. Per-ml figures are simple division of the listed price by the listed size. Black Friday pricing was live on most sites, so regular and sale prices are both shown where they differ.

Beguile's own Canadian prices, for reference (beguile.com storefront with country set to Canada, 2026-11-25): 100ml perfumes CA$110 (Mystique, Goddess, Golden Kiss, Sweet Oud, Blush Bloom) or CA$75 (Passion, Seduction); 20ml oils CA$50; 10ml CA$35; Mini Set CA$105; 3ml samples CA$0. That is CA$1.10/ml on the bestsellers. Shipping policy quotes Canada at 2-10 business days, cost calculated at checkout (https://beguile.com/policies/shipping-policy).

## Takeaways

1. **Beguile is the cheapest per ml of every brand here, by a wide margin.** CA$1.10/ml on a 100ml bottle against CA$2.03-3.94/ml for the four CAD-priced competitors. The Canadian norm is a 50ml bottle at CA$127-197; nobody else sells 100ml at CA$110. Beguile can either advertise the value loudly or has room to raise the Canadian price.
2. **No African-founded fragrance house has a real local presence in Canada.** The two diaspora/African options a Canadian shopper can actually order (Foope, Maison Yusif) price in USD, ship cross-border and give no Canadian duty or delivery detail. Influxious, the Nigerian-heritage Sephora launch of 2026, ships to US addresses only. The "Nigerian fine fragrance, priced in CAD, delivered to your door" slot is open.
3. **The real fight for the wallet is with The 7 Virtues and Kayali.** Both own Beguile's scent territory (vanilla, gourmand, musk, amber), both sell on Sephora Canada's shelf, both push "long-lasting", and both run 10ml travel sizes and CA$5-7 samples. They win on trust and reach (hundreds of reviews per scent; 64.7K and 511.4K TikTok followers), not on price.
4. **Sampling is standard and mostly paid.** Every competitor has a discovery set (CA$37-82.50 or US$25-38) and most sell single samples. Beguile's free 3ml sample is more generous than anything here, but it is not promoted as a Canada offer.
5. **Black Friday depth in Canada is 20-30%.** The 7 Virtues 20% sitewide to 3 Dec, Kayali "up to 30%", Noteworthy (considered, not profiled) 30%. Beguile's "up to 40%" would be the deepest in this set.

## Competitors

### 1. The 7 Virtues

**Why it made the cut:** the Canadian brand a vanilla-loving Beguile customer is most likely to already own; local, on Sephora's shelf and priced in CAD.

- **Positioning:** "Clean, long-lasting perfumes. We are proudly cruelty free and vegan" (site meta description). Mantra "Make Perfume Not War"; oils sourced from nations rebuilding after conflict. Female-founded and family-run (Barb Stegemann, founder and CEO), B Corp certified, Leaping Bunny, hypoallergenic.
- **Range and pricing (CAD storefront):** about 14 eau de parfums, mostly vanilla/gourmand/fruity (Vanilla Woods, Santal Vanille, Amber Vanilla, Buttercream Haze, Strawberry Jam, Cherry Ambition, Candied Lychee, Coconut Sun, Amber Orchid). 50ml CA$127 (CA$2.54/ml); 10ml travel spray CA$43.50 (CA$4.35/ml); 1.7ml sampler CA$7. Layering Discovery Set CA$37; Perfume Layering Discovery Set CA$43; Mini Vanilla Duo CA$43; Best of Vanilla Gift Set CA$134; Layer Cake Collection CA$78. No 100ml size.
- **Offers:** "20% off sitewide until December 3rd" (Vanilla Woods 50ml shown at CA$101.60, was CA$127, with a BFCM countdown). Best of Vanilla Gift Set labelled "Save 37%". 10% off first order for email sign-up. Free rollerball with a CA$70+ purchase after taking the quiz.
- **Shipping:** ships to Canada and the continental US; free standard shipping over $79; parcels ship within 5-7 business days; no returns "for hygienic reasons" per the shipping policy, although the product page says "Free and easy returns within 30 days" (the two statements conflict).
- **Website notes:** fragrance quiz with gift incentive, layering sets pushed hard, merch (tote bags, twillies), store locator. Reviews on every product: Vanilla Woods 4.9/5 from 202 reviews; Blackberry Lily 167; Candied Lychee 147; Clementine Dream 130.
- **Socials:** Instagram @the7virtues (followers not found; profile requires login). TikTok @the7virtues: 64.7K followers, 874.8K likes; bio "Clean, long-lasting fragrances. Canadian and female-founded... Available exclusively at @sephora @sephoracanada and in 19 countries"; playlists by scent (Vanilla Woods 16 posts, Buttercream Haze 23, Strawberry Jam 23), "Layering Combos" (21) and "Meet The Founder" (30); a TikTok LIVE event was scheduled for 26 Nov. Facebook /the7virtues and X @the7virtues are linked; counts not checked. Posting frequency not measured.
- **Strengths:** Sephora Canada distribution; Canadian origin story; deep review counts; full sample-to-full-size ladder; founder-led content.
- **Weak spots:** 50ml only, at more than twice Beguile's per-ml price; "clean/hypoallergenic" positioning rather than performance; 5-7 business days to dispatch; contradictory returns messaging.

### 2. Kayali

- **Shipping:** ships to Canada from its own store; "Free delivery starting from 142 CAD". Delivery times and returns terms not found (shipping and FAQ pages returned 404 on the paths tried).
- **Website notes:** "Find your fragrance" tool, reviews page, award badges on products, #KAYALI UGC wall, date-of-birth sign-up.
- **Socials:** Instagram @kayali (followers not found). TikTok @kayali: 511.4K followers, 5.7M likes; current bio line promotes the Yum Vanilla Cupcake launch ("Have you seen the sprinkles too?"). Posting frequency not measured.

## Where the brand stands

**Price position.** Beguile is the value outlier. At CA$110 for 100ml (CA$1.10/ml) it undercuts The 7 Virtues' sale price per ml (CA$2.03) by almost half and Paraphrase by more than 70%. In absolute ticket, CA$110 sits below The 7 Virtues' regular 50ml (CA$127) and well below Kayali's 50ml (CA$142). Converted loosely, even the USD brands cost more per ml. On small formats Beguile is competitive rather than cheap: 10ml at CA$35 against Kayali CA$39-44 and The 7 Virtues CA$43.50; 20ml oil at CA$50 against OUDIE oil rubs at CA$35-65 (size unknown).

**Gaps Beguile could own.**
- African-founded fine fragrance with a CAD storefront and stated Canadian delivery times. No competitor here offers that combination.
- 100ml at a 50ml price. Nobody else in the CAD group sells 100ml under CA$200.
- Free samples. Competitors charge CA$5-11 per sample.
- "Named for a feeling" plus performance. The 7 Virtues leads with clean and cause, Paraphrase with art, Kayali with layering; none leads with identity plus all-day wear for Black women.

**Threats.**
- Trust gap. The 7 Virtues shows 100-200 reviews per scent and Sephora validation; Kayali has half a million TikTok followers. A Canadian shopper who has never smelled Beguile has little local proof.
- Delivery friction. Local brands dispatch from Ontario or Halifax with free-shipping thresholds of CA$79-150. Beguile's shipping cost is "calculated at checkout" with a 2-10 day window and no stated threshold or duty position.
- Influxious opening international shipping or entering Sephora Canada would put a Nigerian-heritage brand with US press on the same shelf as Kayali.
- A very low price next to CA$127-290 competitors may read as "not luxury" without framing.

## Recommended moves

1. **Publish a Canada-specific delivery promise.** State a flat CAD shipping rate, a free-shipping threshold (competitors sit at CA$79-150; CA$150 would cover one bottle plus an oil or 10ml), where the parcel ships from, and whether duties are included. This is the single clearest advantage local brands hold today.
2. **Lead Canadian ads and landing pages with the per-ml comparison.** "100ml for CA$110" against 50ml at CA$127-197 elsewhere, paired with verified-buyer longevity quotes. Consider testing CA$125-135 on the bestsellers after Black Friday; Beguile would still be the cheapest per ml in the market.
3. **Make the free 3ml sample the Canadian acquisition offer.** Competitors charge for samples and discovery sets. A "try Mystique or Goddess free, pay shipping" offer, followed by a credit toward a full bottle (as Influxious and Noteworthy do with US$25 vouchers), fits how this market already buys.
4. **Seed the Toronto/GTA, Calgary and Edmonton Nigerian diaspora in person.** OUDIE's workshops and fragrance bars show the local playbook. A pop-up or stockist at an Afro-Canadian market or boutique, plus 10-15 Canadian diaspora creators, would build the local proof Beguile lacks.
5. **Hold the "up to 40%" Black Friday line in Canada but anchor it.** It is deeper than every competitor (20-30%). Show the CAD regular price beside the sale price and keep bestsellers at a shallower discount so the brand does not look cheap next to Kayali and The 7 Virtues.

## Gaps in this research

- **Instagram follower counts were not obtained for any of the six.** Instagram redirected to a login page and rate-limited fetches, and search results returned no counts. All are recorded as "not found". TikTok counts were read directly from profile pages.
- **Posting frequency, content mix and engagement** were not measured: TikTok video grids did not load and Instagram was closed. Only bios, playlists and totals are reported.
- **Sephora Canada** blocked access (HTTP 403), so Sephora.ca listings and prices for The 7 Virtues and Kayali were not checked. The 7 Virtues' Sephora Canada distribution comes from its own TikTok bio; Kayali's is general knowledge, unverified here.
- **OUDIE bottle sizes** are not stated on its product pages; the per-ml figure is conditional on an unconfirmed 50ml. Founder name not found.
- **Maison Yusif and Foope**: actual shipping cost, duties and delivery time to a Canadian address were not tested at checkout. Maison Yusif never names Canada.
- **Kayali** delivery times and returns for Canada not found. Paraphrase travel-size volume not captured.
- **Only four of the six price in CAD.** Foope and Maison Yusif are cross-border options with weak Canadian traction; they are included because no African or diaspora-led fragrance house with a stronger Canadian footprint was found. Treat the first four as the solid local set.
- No exchange rate was applied; USD prices are left in USD.
- Semrush traffic data, paid-ad libraries and press coverage were not pulled in this run.
