# United Kingdom: competitor report (2026-11-25)

All prices, follower counts and offers were seen on 2026-11-25 unless stated. "Not found" means the figure could not be confirmed; nothing here is estimated. Beguile's own UK figures used for comparison: 100ml perfume £85 (Passion and Seduction £60), 20ml oil £30, 10ml travel size £20, Mini Set £75 (https://beguile.com/collections/all?country=GB); Instagram 34.4K followers (https://www.instagram.com/beguilebyomaa/); UK delivery 4–8 business days, rate calculated at checkout (https://beguile.com/policies/shipping-policy).

## Takeaways

1. **Beguile's £85 for 100ml is the lowest regular per-ml price of the seven brands except Shay & Blue, but this week it is being undercut.** Regular prices per ml: Shay & Blue £0.80, Beguile £0.85, Sunnamusk £0.90, Kayali £1.10, Scent of Africa £1.28, Gallivant £1.50, Maya Njie £2.10. Black Friday changes the order: Shay & Blue is at £0.40–£0.56/ml plus a free 100ml over £80, and Kayali is £0.77/ml at Space NK. Beguile's "up to 40% off" was still an early-access sign-up on the day of this research, while three of the six competitors already had live offers.
2. **Nobody in the UK owns "long-lasting fine fragrance by a Nigerian woman, for women like me".** The two African-heritage brands here sit elsewhere: Maya Njie is quiet, artisanal, Scandinavian-leaning niche at £105 for 50ml; Scent of Africa is a Ghanaian prestige line sold through one London pharmacy at £128 with no UK direct store. Neither speaks to the diaspora customer in Beguile's voice. The space is open.
3. **The real fight for Beguile's scent territory is with Sunnamusk and Kayali, not with African brands.** Sunnamusk sells amber/musk/oud EDPs at £90 per 100ml and perfume oils at £29 for 15ml, has UK stores, 8,830 Trustpilot reviews and 88.5K Instagram followers. Kayali owns vanilla/gourmand/musk on UK social (2M Instagram, 511K TikTok) and is on the shelf at Space NK, Boots, Sephora UK, Harrods and Selfridges.
4. **UK delivery terms are Beguile's weakest point against this set.** Every competitor with a UK store publishes a flat rate and a free-shipping threshold (Sunnamusk free over £50, Shay & Blue free over £60 with free returns, Gallivant £5 or free over £120, Space NK free over £25 for Kayali). Beguile shows "calculated at checkout" and 4–8 business days.
5. **Low-cost trial is standard here.** Sunnamusk mini discovery sets are £14.99, Kayali's layering set is £25, Gallivant's 12-scent set is £35 with a £30 voucher back, Shay & Blue offers a first 10ml for £1 by subscription. Beguile's free 3ml samples and £75 Mini Set are competitive, but there is no paid, redeemable discovery set between them.

## Competitors

### 1. Maya Njie

Made the cut because it is the best-known African-heritage perfume house based in the UK, and is sold at Liberty.

- **Positioning:** "Perfume shaped by my Swedish and West African heritage. Personal recollections told through scent." Self-taught perfumer, founded 2016, studio in Shoreditch. Memory and family photographs, not glamour or performance.
- **Range and pricing:** Eight core EDPs (Nordic Cedar, Vanilj, Les Fleurs, Tobak, Tropica, Voyeur Verde, Syrus, Aethi Opum) at £105 for 50ml (£2.10/ml) and £32 for 7.5ml (£4.27/ml). Treemoss £21/5ml, £64/30ml, £153/100ml. Liquid Amber £145/50ml. Discovery Set £48 for 8 x 1.5ml, sold out on the day. Refills and gift vouchers offered.
- **Offers:** No Black Friday offer seen. 10% off code for newsletter sign-up; 10% off a bottle after buying the discovery set.
- **Shipping:** UK dispatch within 2 working days by Royal Mail 2nd class signed for; UK rate not shown on the policy page (not found). Returns within 14 days, unused, at the customer's cost.
- **Website notes:** Shopify. Bespoke perfume workshops (2.5 hours) at the studio. Short film "The Invisible Places We Carry" on the homepage. No on-site reviews seen. Supports The Gambia Academy and Ecologi.
- **Socials:** Instagram @maya.njie.perfumes, 32.3K followers, 770 posts. TikTok @mayanjieperfumes, 4,201 followers, 7,421 likes. Facebook /mayanjieperfumes and X @MayaNjiePerfume, follower counts not found (login wall). Content is founder-led and editorial; highlights are Workshops, Film, Vogue. Posting frequency and engagement not found.
- **Other signals:** Stocked at Liberty London (stated in the Instagram bio). "Featured in" Vogue, Financial Times, New York Times, Monocle, Elle. The stockists page rendered empty.
- **Strengths:** Press and Liberty credibility; clear founder story; workshops create a physical touchpoint in London.
- **Weak spots:** 2.5 times Beguile's price per ml; no 100ml in the core range; discovery set out of stock in peak gifting week; small TikTok; no reviews on site; not positioned on longevity.

Perfume oils £29/15ml (£1.93/ml) and £45/30ml (£1.50/ml); White Musk oil from £21/10ml. Mini discovery sets £14.99. Gift sets £80–£125. Also bakhoor, burners, candles, room sprays.
- **Offers:** 20% off the Exclusive Collection (25% for Sunnamusk Society members). "Buy 2 get 3rd 50% off" on perfume oils. Selected lines 25% off. £5 off first order for joining the loyalty scheme.
- **Shipping:** UK standard free over £50 (homepage banner says £60, members £50; the policy page says £50). 2nd class £3.95, 1st class £5.95, express £9.95. Returns within 30 days, sealed items only, customer pays postage; homepage advertises extended 45-day gift returns.

## Where the brand stands

**Price position.** At £85 for 100ml (£0.85/ml), Beguile sits second-cheapest at regular price, between Shay & Blue (£0.80) and Sunnamusk (£0.90), and well under Kayali (£1.10), Scent of Africa (£1.28), Gallivant (£1.50) and Maya Njie (£2.10). Its 20ml oil at £30 (£1.50/ml) matches Sunnamusk's 30ml oil per ml (£1.50) and beats its 15ml (£1.93) and Gallivant's 10ml (£2.80). Beguile is priced as accessible, not niche. The UK price is also not a simple conversion of the naira price: £85 against ₦86,000 in Nigeria.

**Gaps Beguile could own.**
- A woman-founded Nigerian fine fragrance house speaking to British-Nigerian and wider Black British women. No competitor found does this in the UK.
- Feeling-led names (Mystique, Goddess, Seduction) against note-led or place-led naming everywhere else in the set.
- Longevity as a headline claim with proof. Sunnamusk gets it from reviews but does not lead with it; the niche houses do not claim it at all.
- 100ml as standard at under £1/ml. The niche houses sell 30ml and 50ml.

**Threats.**
- Sunnamusk: same territory and price, with stores, 8,830 Trustpilot reviews and a loyalty scheme.
- Kayali: on every major UK beauty shelf and at £0.77/ml on promotion.
- Shay & Blue: trains UK buyers to expect a 100ml bottle for about £50 and free returns.
- Trust: Beguile has no UK Trustpilot presence, UK stockist or published UK delivery rate that this research could find, and the default storefront opens in naira.

## Recommended moves

1. **Publish UK delivery terms that match the market:** a flat tracked rate and a free-delivery threshold just above one bottle (the set clusters at £50–£60), shown in a site banner for GBP visitors. State where UK orders ship from.
2. **Open the Black Friday offer to UK buyers now, and frame it per bottle.** Competitors are live at 20–60% off. If margin is tight, copy the mechanic rather than the depth: a free 10ml or oil with a 100ml, as Shay & Blue does with its free 100ml over £80.
3. **Add a paid, redeemable discovery set for the UK** (for example all seven perfumes in 3ml, with the price credited against a 100ml). Gallivant's £35 set with £30 back and Sunnamusk's £14.99 minis show the price range that works here. The free 3ml samples stay as an add-on.
4. **Build UK proof:** collect UK reviews on Trustpilot or show UK-tagged verified reviews on site, and turn the "London event" in the Instagram highlights into a recurring pop-up with creator content from British-Nigerian women. Wear-test longevity content is the format to prioritise, since that is the claim Sunnamusk's customers make for it.
5. **Lead UK ads and landing pages with the comparison Beguile wins:** 100ml fine fragrance at £85 against £105 for 50ml (Maya Njie) and £110 for 100ml (Kayali), under a founder-led, feeling-first message none of the six uses.
