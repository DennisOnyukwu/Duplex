# United States: competitor report (2026-11-25)

All prices, follower counts and offers below were seen on 2026-11-25 unless a different date is given. Prices are USD list prices from each brand's own US storefront; "sale" prices are the Black Friday prices live that day. Follower counts are read from the public profile page itself unless labelled otherwise.

For reference, Beguile's own US storefront (https://beguile.com/collections/all?country=US) showed 100ml perfume at $120 ($1.20/ml), Mini Set $100, 10ml $30 (sold out), free 3ml samples, and only three scents (Mystique, Goddess, Golden Kiss). Instagram @beguilebyomaa: 34.4K followers.

## Takeaways

1. **Beguile is the cheapest per ml of the seven, and nobody in the US can tell.** At $1.20/ml for 100ml it undercuts every competitor's list price (range $1.29 to $3.00/ml). But the US norm is a 50ml bottle at $79 to $150, so Beguile's $120 ticket sits at the top of the shelf by sticker price while offering the best value. The value story is not told anywhere on the US pages.
2. **The "Nigerian heritage fine fragrance" position in the US now has a well-funded owner: Influxious.** Launched at Sephora on 27 July 2026 by Nigerian-born creator Funmi Monet, it sells four scents at $150/50ml, is in 11 Sephora stores, and uses the same "scent as identity" language Beguile does ("heritage becomes expression & scent becomes identity"). It is the most direct threat in this market and charges 2.5 times Beguile's price per ml.
3. **The US shopper sees a thinner Beguile than the Nigerian shopper.** The US catalogue lists 8 products and three scents. Sweet Oud, Blush Bloom, Passion, Seduction and all three perfume oils are missing, and the 10ml is sold out. Every competitor has a paid discovery set ($25 to $39) and a 10 to 15ml travel size in stock. Oud and vanilla, Beguile's strongest territory against Kayali and Forvr Mood, is not purchasable in the US.
4. **Black Friday is already live at every competitor, at 20 to 30% off; Beguile is still collecting emails for "up to 40%".** Influxious (20% off 50ml), Brown Girl Jane (up to 30%, no code), Kayali (up to 30%, tiered), Forvr Mood (seven days of one-day deals) and Moodeaux (half-price and free travel-size add-ons) were all selling on 25 November. Beguile's headline discount is deeper, but it is arriving last.
5. **Sephora is the trust signal in this market, and four of the six have it.** Influxious, Forvr Mood, Brown Girl Jane and Kayali all point to Sephora. Beguile cannot match that soon, so reviews, samples and returns have to do that work; its 14-day unopened returns policy is already more generous than Brown Girl Jane and Kimberly New York (no returns at all).

## Competitors

### 1. Influxious

Chosen because it is the closest substitute in the market: a Nigerian-heritage fine fragrance house, founder-led, aimed at the same diaspora and Black American customer.

- **Positioning:** "Luxury fragrance that expresses every dimension of you." Instagram bio: "A modern fragrance house where heritage becomes expression & scent becomes identity." Founder Funmi Monet was born in Nigeria and raised in the US, trained at ISIPCA in Paris and is a Sephora Accelerate fellow. Notes reference Nigeria directly (Nigerian ginger in Gold Standard; press describes High Life as inspired by Lagos and zobo).
- **Range and pricing:** Four eaux de parfum (Gold Standard, High Life, Magic Hour, Well Loved). 50ml $150 list ($3.00/ml); 10ml $35 ($3.50/ml). Discovery Set 4 x 2ml $25 with a $25 voucher toward a full bottle (sold out on the day). Travel Collection Set $115 (was $140); Gold Standard Gifting Set $205 (was $255).
- **Offers:** Black Friday live: 20% off every 50ml bottle ($120). 10% off for email/SMS sign-up. Free complimentary sample (valued $7) unlocked at a spend threshold.
- **Shipping:** US street addresses only; no international. Free over $100, otherwise $8 flat. Ships in 1 to 2 business days, delivery 3 to 5.
- **Website notes:** Shopify. Star ratings on every product (Gold Standard 4.8 from 70 reviews; Discovery Set 4.9 from 64), mostly via the Shop app. "Pairs well with" layering prompts. Store locator lists 11 Sephora doors (Atlanta, Aventura, Dallas, Houston, Las Vegas, Los Angeles, three in New York, Rocklin, Santa Clara). No quiz.
- **Socials:** Instagram @influxious.co 20.4K followers, 137 posts. TikTok @influxious.co 10.8K followers, 84.6K likes. YouTube @Influxious 191 subscribers, 35 videos (one short film per scent, 84 to 178 views each). Facebook page linked; follower count not found. Content is founder-led: the brand accounts all sign off "by @funmimonet", whose personal audience is the real engine. Posting frequency could not be measured (feeds not readable without login).
- **Other signals:** Sephora online launch 27 July 2026, stores from late September 2026. Press in Fashionista, Beauty Independent, TheGrio, AfroTech, Puck. Search snippets of that press report the founder saying sales passed $1 million in the first three weeks, and conflicting funding accounts ($225,000 self-funded versus about $500,000 raised); these are third-party snippets, not confirmed on the articles themselves.
- **Strengths:** Sephora distribution and physical testers; founder with an existing fragrance audience; Nigerian story told with craft credentials; strong launch press.
- **Weak spots:** Most expensive per ml of the set by a wide margin; only four scents and 50ml as the largest size; discovery set sold out during Black Friday week; brand-owned social accounts are still small; does not ship outside the US.

## Where the brand stands

**Price position.** List price per ml, lowest first: Beguile $1.20 (100ml) · Kimberly New York $1.29 (100ml) · Kayali $1.50 (100ml) · Forvr Mood $1.58 (50ml) · Moodeaux $1.96 (50ml) · Brown Girl Jane $2.04 (50ml) · Influxious $3.00 (50ml). By ticket price the order nearly reverses: Forvr Mood $79, Moodeaux $98, Brown Girl Jane $102, Beguile $120, Kimberly New York $129, Influxious and Kayali $150. Beguile is the value leader that looks mid-to-premium on the shelf. Under current Black Friday pricing, Brown Girl Jane ($71.40) and Forvr Mood ($75 for 50ml plus 10ml) sit far below Beguile's ticket until Beguile's own sale opens.

**Gaps the brand could own.**
- *Big bottle, lasting scent, fair price.* Only Kimberly New York and Kayali sell 100ml, and neither leads with value. "Twice the perfume for less" is unclaimed.
- *Made in Nigeria and Dubai, not just inspired by it.* Influxious and Forvr Mood are American brands with Nigerian founders. Beguile is the only one actually based in Nigeria with Dubai perfumery, and it delivers in both countries from its own store, which matters to diaspora gifting.
- *Perfume oils.* Only Moodeaux has an oil format and it is out of stock. Beguile's three 20ml oils are not offered to US shoppers at all.
- *Oud for the diaspora customer.* Kayali charges $140 for 50ml of oud; Forvr Mood folds oud into gourmands. Sweet Oud at Beguile's pricing would be the best-value oud in the set, but it is not in the US catalogue.

**Threats.**
- Influxious occupying "Nigerian heritage, scent as identity" with Sephora shelves and founder reach before Beguile is known in the US.
- Forvr Mood's price and community programme capturing the same diaspora customer at $79, and its presence in Nigeria through Beauty Hut Africa.
- No way to smell Beguile in the US. Four of six rivals have Sephora testers; all six sell a discovery set. Beguile's free 3ml sample is the right tool but covers only three scents.
- A late Black Friday: competitors' sales were live on 25 November while Beguile was still pre-launch.

## Recommended moves

1. **Open the full range to the US store, starting with Sweet Oud and the three oils.** These are the products with the least direct competition here. Restock the 10ml and add the remaining scents so the US shopper sees the same house the Nigerian shopper does.
2. **Sell a US discovery set at $25 to $30 with a credit toward a full bottle.** That is the exact mechanic used by Influxious and Moodeaux, and Brown Girl Jane's 2,138 reviews on its set show it is the entry product in this market. Keep the free 3ml sample as an add-on rather than the only trial route.
3. **Put the per-ml comparison on the US product page.** A single line such as "100ml for $120; most niche houses charge $100 to $150 for 50ml" turns the sticker price into the selling point. Consider a 50ml at around $75 to $80 to meet Forvr Mood's ticket for first-time buyers.
4. **Tell the origin story the others cannot.** Lead US content with Nigerian-founded, Dubai-composed, and ship-to-family-in-Nigeria gifting. Influxious has claimed the heritage language, so compete on being the original article, with the founder on camera; every strong account in this set is founder-led.
5. **Show US-specific trust on the page:** delivery times and shipping cost for the US up front (competitors quote a free-shipping threshold of $65 to $100; Beguile shows none), reviews from US buyers, and the 14-day returns policy stated near the buy button, since two rivals accept no returns.
