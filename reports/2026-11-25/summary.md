# Beguile competitor summary: all markets (25 November 2026)

First run. Twenty-nine competitor profiles across five countries: six each for Nigeria, the UK, the USA and Canada, and five for Ghana, where a sixth credible brand could not be found. Each market has its own report in this folder with full profiles and sources.

## Headline findings

1. **Beguile is the cheapest, or level with the cheapest, per ml in every market it ships to.** A 100ml bottle costs less per ml than every competitor in the USA and Canada, is second-cheapest in the UK, and is level with the cheapest in Nigeria. The gap is widest abroad: competitors there sell 50ml for about what Beguile charges for 100ml. None of Beguile's storefronts says so.
2. **Black Friday started without Beguile.** On 25 November, five of six US competitors, three of six in the UK and two of six in Canada were already discounting 20–60%. Beguile was still collecting early-access emails. In Nigeria and Ghana almost nobody was discounting, and several Nigerian rivals were sold out.
3. **Outside Nigeria, the missing pieces are trust and delivery, not price.** Local rivals publish flat shipping rates and free-delivery thresholds, show hundreds of reviews, and sit on Sephora, Space NK or Liberty shelves. Beguile shows "calculated at checkout".
4. **Paid discovery sets are how this category brings in new buyers abroad.** Every US competitor sells one ($25–39), and UK sets run £14.99–35, often with the price credited toward a bottle. Beguile's free 3ml sample is a stronger offer but covers only three scents.
5. **The US storefront shows a much smaller Beguile.** It lists three scents. Sweet Oud, Blush Bloom, Passion, Seduction and all three oils are missing, and those are the products with the least competition there.
6. **One brand is claiming Beguile's story in North America.** Influxious (US, Nigerian-born founder) uses near-identical "scent as identity" positioning, sells at Sephora, and charges 2.5 times Beguile's price per ml. It does not yet ship to Canada.
7. **No Nigerian woman-founded fine fragrance house has a local presence in the UK, Canada or Ghana.** That position is open in all three.

## Recommended moves

Ranked by how many markets the evidence comes from.

1. **Say the per-ml advantage out loud on every foreign storefront.** "100ml for $120 / £85 / CA$110" against 50ml at $79–150, £105 and CA$127–197. Evidence: price tables in the USA, UK and Canada reports.
2. **Publish delivery terms per country:** a flat rate, a free-delivery threshold, where it ships from and whether duties are included. Competitors' thresholds are $65–100 (USA), £50–60 (UK) and CA$79–150 (Canada).
3. **Open Black Friday now in the USA, UK and Canada, and keep it shallow in Nigeria.** Abroad, rivals are already live. In Nigeria nobody is discounting and several are out of stock, so "in stock, delivered before Christmas" is worth more than depth.
4. **Add a paid discovery set with the price credited toward a full bottle,** and keep the free 3ml as an add-on. Evidence: all six US competitors, plus Gallivant and Sunnamusk in the UK.
5. **List the full range on the US store,** starting with Sweet Oud and the three oils. Only one US competitor has an oil, and it is out of stock.
6. **Build local proof abroad:** reviews from buyers in each country, and pop-ups or a stockist in London, Toronto and one US city. Trust, not price, is what The 7 Virtues, Kayali and Sunnamusk win on.
7. **In Nigeria, answer Jan Deux and Perfumeology directly:** a gifting layer for December (Jan Deux sells wrapping and gift bags) and a retention mechanic (Perfumeology charges ₦5,000 a month for a 15% member discount).
8. **Treat Ghana as a small-format test before committing.** Cedi pricing, Mobile Money at checkout and one-to-three-day delivery are the entry requirements; lead with the Mini Set, 10ml and samples.
9. **Start a TikTok account.** It is the second-largest audience for most competitors in every market.

## Gaps

- **Posting frequency, content mix and engagement were not measured for any brand.** Instagram and TikTok show profile headers but not post feeds without a login. The reports carry follower counts only.
- **Many Instagram counts are missing:** all six in Canada, three in Nigeria, and several in Ghana and the UK.
- **No traffic or keyword data.** The Semrush account had no API units left.
- **Paid ads were not checked** (Meta Ad Library) in any market.
- **Ghana has five profiles, not six.**
- **Some sets are uneven.** Kayali is far larger than an independent house and is included for scent territory. Maison Yusif is a weak fit in Nigeria and Canada. Majestic Roobee sells some designer-inspired oils.
- **Three hero prices rest on an assumption:** Kayali's UK regular price (derived from a promotional price), Toke Makinwa Beauty's bottle size (from a third-party review), and OUDIE's bottle size (not stated on its site).
- **Shipping costs to each country were not tested at checkout,** for Beguile or for the cross-border competitors.
