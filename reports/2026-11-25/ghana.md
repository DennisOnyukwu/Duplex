# Ghana: competitor report (2026-11-25)

All prices, follower counts and claims below were seen on 2026-11-25 unless a different date is given. "Not found" means the figure could not be confirmed; nothing here is estimated.

**Only five competitors are profiled, not six.** Ghana's own-brand fragrance scene is small: local press counts about six SMEs making perfume in the country, and most of what shoppers buy is imported designer and Arabian perfume sold through retailers, Jiji listings and Instagram resellers, which the brand profile excludes. The candidates that did not make the cut are listed under "Gaps in this research".

## Takeaways

1. **Beguile would land in an empty price slot.** Converted at about ₦121 to ₵1 (Wise mid-market, page undated), Beguile's ₦76,000–86,000 100ml is roughly ₵630–710, about ₵6.3–7.1 per ml. That is just above Hareem Istanbul (₵560 for 100ml) and about half of Maison Yusif (₵1,350 for 100ml), Sanhwe (₵1,380) and Scent of Africa (₵1,952). No local house sells a 100ml fine fragrance between ₵600 and ₵1,300. This is a conversion, not a Beguile cedi price: Beguile has none, and does not ship to Ghana.
2. **Maison Yusif owns the "African niche house" story in Ghana.** 108K Instagram followers, 51.3K on TikTok, three stores (two in Accra, one in Kumasi), 48-hour delivery, constant national press. Its weight is in men's and unisex scents: only three of 25 products are filed under Women, and its hero women's scent is out of stock until December 2026.
3. **Every local competitor sells on heritage; none sells on feeling.** Maison Yusif (Ghanaian materials), Scent of Africa (African mythology), Sanhwe (Twi names), Talata (African ingredients). Beguile's "scent as identity, named for a feeling" and its women-first range are not occupied here.
4. **Delivery is the entry ticket.** Everyone profiled delivers inside Ghana in one to three days, and three have walk-in stores. Beguile lists no Ghana shipping, so today a Ghanaian shopper cannot buy it at all.
5. **Small sizes are how Ghanaians enter a brand.** Sanhwe sells from 3ml at ₵120, Hareem from 12ml at ₵90, Maison Yusif from 30ml at ₵650. Beguile's 10ml, 3ml samples and Mini Set fit this habit well.

## Competitors

### 1. Maison Yusif

Why it made the cut: the best-known Ghanaian fragrance house, selling direct online in cedis with stores in Accra and Kumasi.

**Positioning.** "Be felt before you are seen." Award-winning niche perfumery, hand-blended in small batches in Accra by founder and perfumer Yusif Meizongo Jnr, established 2017. Leans on Ghanaian heritage materials and on performance: the Scent of a Queen page claims "32h fabric, 16h skin" and "enormous" sillage.

**Range and pricing.** 25 products: men's, unisex and three women's scents (Scent of a Queen, The Empress, Take Me To Paris), limited editions, body care, discovery sets. The site shows cedis to Ghana visitors and dollars elsewhere, at exactly 10 cedis to the dollar on every item checked.
- Scent of a Queen (extrait, women): ₵650–₵1,350 in the Ghana view; in the dollar view 30ml $65, 50ml $80, 100ml $135. The 50ml cedi price was not seen directly.
- The Vatican (unisex): ₵350–₵1,500. Bad Influence (unisex, bestseller): ₵650–₵1,600. Deeper Than Words (men): ₵550–₵1,550.
- The Empress and Take Me To Paris (women): $165 each in the dollar view; cedi view not checked.
- The Apex (men): ₵2,800, was ₵2,900. Black Excellence (men, limited): $655.
- A Discovery Of Self (10 × 8ml): ₵1,550, sold out. Petit Atelier set: $50.

**Offers.** Pre-order on Scent of a Queen, The Vatican and The Apex: "save $10", stock expected December 2026. "Unlocked Complimentary Shipping" banner in the cart, no threshold stated. No Black Friday offer seen on the site.

**Shipping.** Within Ghana in about 48 hours; United States 5–7 business days; rest of world 7–20. Fee calculated at checkout. Returns only for unopened items, requested within 48 hours of delivery; none for scent preference.

**Website notes.** Scent Finder quiz, verified-purchase reviews on product pages (Scent of a Queen: 22 reviews, 5.0), order tracking, perfumer's note on each product, layering suggestions. Stores: Kokomlemle (Accra), East Legon (Accra), Asokwa (Kumasi).

**Socials.**
- Instagram @maisonyusif: 108K followers, 2,293 posts (profile page). Posting frequency and content mix not found; the profile would not load past the header.
- TikTok @maisonyusiffragrance: 51.3K followers, 723.5K likes (profile page). Videos did not load.
- Facebook facebook.com/maisonyusif and X twitter.com/maisonyusif: follower counts not found (login wall).

**Strengths.** Largest following of any local house; physical stores in two cities; steady press (Paris Perfume Week, "Best Niche Fragrance House 2026 – Africa" from Acquisition International, The Beast launch in July 2026); strong longevity claims; full size ladder.

**Weak spots.** Hero women's scent and two other bestsellers are out of stock on pre-order; thin women's range; 48-hour returns window; awards and sales claims come mostly from the brand's own press releases; discovery set sold out.

### 2. Scent of Africa

Why it made the cut: the premium "made in Ghana" house, widely stocked in Accra and priced above everyone else.

**Positioning.** "An emblem of olfactory jewels from the continent." Fine fragrance house founded by Tanal Ghandour (Ghandour Cosmetics), headquartered in Accra, fragrances composed in France. Each scent is tied to an African legend. Design awards quoted on its profiles: Pentawards bronze 2022, NY Product Design Awards gold 2023.

**Range and pricing.** Nefee, Rakh, Laïka, Hagé, Bellua, Bézi, Fik, Gleti, plus Anancy (men) and Assaye (women, vanilla), both launched June 2026. Sizes 100ml, 50ml, 10ml.
- Nefee EDP at Scentopia Ghana: 50ml ₵1,280, 100ml ₵1,952. This is a retailer price.
- The brand's own product page shows Assaye at "GHC 75.00" for all sizes, which looks like a placeholder; treat as unreliable.
- Outside Ghana: £64 for 50ml at Lux Afrique Boutique; Ksh 9,700 for 50ml at Lintons Beauty (Kenya).

**Offers.** None on the brand site. Scentopia runs a sitewide "promotions up to 50% off" banner; whether Scent of Africa is included was not confirmed. Lintons (Kenya) has 20% off some gift sets.

**Shipping.** The brand's own site has a cart but no shipping or returns information. In Ghana the practical route is Scentopia: free standard delivery within Accra, same-day express for orders before 4pm (charged).

**Website notes.** Dated Wix site (footer says 2021), no reviews, no samples or discovery set listed, store locator covering 16 countries. Press reported a new e-commerce platform due later in 2026; not seen live.

**Socials.**
- Instagram @scentofafrica: 34K followers, 483 posts (profile page). Bio promotes Anancy and Assaye.
- TikTok @scentofafrica: 13.6K followers, 37.1K likes (profile page). Playlists by scent; the largest is "Assaye X Anancy" with 5 posts.
- Facebook facebook.com/scentofafrica, X twitter.com/scentofafrica, YouTube channel: counts not found.

**Strengths.** Distribution: Scentopia's Accra Mall flagship, luxury perfumeries, Kotoka airport duty-free, 16 countries; polished packaging and storytelling; a new vanilla women's scent in Beguile's territory.

**Weak spots.** Highest price per ml of the five; weak direct-to-consumer site with a visibly wrong price; no reviews, samples or quiz; low social activity for its size (483 Instagram posts in roughly a decade).

### 3. Hareem Istanbul (Ghana)

Why it made the cut: a single-brand house with a Ghana store, a Ghana site in cedis and the price point closest to Beguile.

**Positioning.** "Made in Istanbul, loved in Accra. Scent your story." Turkish house selling body perfume, body mists, home fragrance and skincare; the Ghana site counts 116 scents.

**Range and pricing.** Women's perfumes Enigma, Chaos, Illusion, Narcist: 100ml ₵560, 50ml ₵370, Enigma 12ml ₵90. Signature Women 50ml ₵232.80 (sold out). Oud collection of four; prices not captured. Body mists ₵80–₵180. Diffusers ₵220–₵250.

**Offers.** None running. Newsletter promises "private offers".

**Shipping.** Dispatch rider (Yango) from the nearest store, Accra in 1–3 days, nationwide delivery; no fixed fee, the rider is paid on delivery. Free pickup at Shop 11, Marina Shopping Mall, Spintex. Pay by card (Paystack), Mobile Money or bank transfer. Returns within 14 days, unopened.

**Website notes.** Four-question scent finder, shop by mood, shop by price band, journal, WhatsApp concierge. No customer reviews seen.

**Socials.**

- Parent accounts (X @HareemIstanbul, YouTube @HareemIstanbul, Facebook Hareemscent): counts not found. No Ghana TikTok account found.

**Strengths.** Cheapest 100ml of the five; cedi pricing and local payment methods; walk-in store; wide gifting range.

**Weak spots.** Small Ghana following; a home-fragrance-led catalogue (59 of its products) dilutes the fine-fragrance story; no reviews; delivery cost is unknown until the rider arrives.

**Gaps Beguile could own.**
- A women-first fine fragrance house. Local leaders skew to men's and unisex scents.
- The ₵600–₵1,300 band for a 100ml with a niche story and reviews behind it.
- Feeling-led naming and identity, against four competitors all telling a heritage story.
- Verified reviews and a quiz together. Only Maison Yusif has both.

**Threats.**
- Maison Yusif's reach and home advantage. It will have Scent of a Queen back in stock in December 2026.
- Cheap Arabian imports (Lattafa and similar at roughly ₵220–₵400 per 100ml on Ghanaian listings, per https://tezeon.com/blog/best-perfume-women-price-ghana-2025) set what many shoppers expect a "long-lasting oud or vanilla" to cost.
- Landed cost. Shipping from Nigeria plus duty could push Beguile out of the open price band.
- A Nigerian brand has no automatic pull in Ghana, where "made in Ghana" is a selling point for three of the five.

## Recommended moves

1. **Open Ghana with a small-format offer before full bottles.** Lead with the Mini Set, 10ml and 3ml samples, priced in cedis, shipped from Lagos or held with an Accra partner. It matches how Sanhwe, Hareem and Maison Yusif bring buyers in, and keeps shipping cost low.
2. **Set cedi prices to hold the open band.** Aim for a landed 100ml price around ₵700–₵900: clearly above Hareem, clearly below Maison Yusif. Test whether duty and courier costs allow it before announcing Ghana shipping.
3. **Take Mobile Money and Paystack at checkout and promise a delivery window.** All five competitors deliver in one to three days; a week-long cross-border wait with card-only payment would lose the sale.
4. **Aim content at women in Accra while the local leader is out of stock.** Maison Yusif's women's hero returns in December 2026; Mystique and Goddess with Ghanaian creators, and the scent quiz, can fill that space now.
5. **Consider a stockist rather than only direct sales.** Scent of Africa reaches Ghanaian shoppers almost entirely through Scentopia and mall perfumeries. A counter or pop-up in Accra would give Beguile the try-before-buying step that its samples provide online.
