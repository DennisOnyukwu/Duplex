# Nigeria: competitor report (2026-11-25)

All prices, follower counts and site observations were read on 2026-11-25 unless a different date is given. "Not found" means the figure could not be confirmed; nothing here is estimated. Per-ml figures are simple division of the listed price by the listed size.

Beguile benchmark used throughout (beguile.com, 2026-11-25): 100ml perfume ₦76,000–86,000 (₦760–860/ml), 20ml oil ₦38,000 (₦1,900/ml), 10ml ₦22,000, Mini Set ₦86,000, free 3ml samples, Instagram @beguilebyomaa 34.4K followers / 427 posts (instagram.com/beguilebyomaa).

## Takeaways

1. **Beguile is the cheapest per ml among the Nigerian houses that sell a branded, original fine fragrance, and it is not close.** At ₦860/ml for a 100ml, it sits level with Perfumeology (₦800/ml) and far below Jan Deux (₦1,800–2,200/ml on 50ml bottles), Yerwa Secrets (₦1,800/ml on 100ml) and Toke Makinwa Beauty (₦1,740–1,885/ml). There is room to hold price through Black Friday rather than cut deep, or to add a premium tier.
2. **The newest threat is Jan Deux, not the established names.** Launched around September 2025, it already shows 19K Instagram followers (Beguile: 34.4K after five years), a Lekki shop, gourmand/oud-vanilla scents squarely in Beguile's territory, and gifting add-ons (wrapping, vanity bag, discovery box). Its weak spots are stock (Eclipse, Rivage and the 3x10ml set are sold out) and a thin site with no shipping policy.
3. **Nobody else in this set matches Beguile's low-risk trial.** Beguile gives 3ml samples free; Yerwa charges ₦40,000 for 4x2ml and ₦80,000 for 8x2ml, Jan Deux ₦100,000 for 4x10ml, Toke Makinwa ₦29,000–36,250 for 10ml. Sampling is an advantage Beguile can advertise far harder.
4. **Competitors are selling out and leaving demand on the table before the holidays.** Toke Makinwa's full bottles of Dive, Intense, Faya and Oud Rush are out of stock on its Nigerian site; Jan Deux's Eclipse and Rivage are sold out; Yerwa's 50ml sizes are mostly unavailable. In-stock, fast delivery is a message that will land right now.
5. **Retention and physical presence are where rivals are ahead.** Perfumeology runs a paid membership (₦5,000/month for 15% off), Majestic Roobee has stores in several cities and a perfume studio in Abuja, Toke Makinwa is stocked by Seinde Signature's store network, Jan Deux has a Lekki shop. Beguile's presence is online plus pop-ups.

### 3. Yerwa Secrets

## Where the brand stands

**Price position.** Per ml on full bottles, lowest to highest: Beguile ₦760–860 (100ml) and Perfumeology ₦800 (100ml); Majestic Roobee ₦800–1,000 (30–50ml); Jan Deux ₦1,650–2,200; Toke Makinwa ₦1,740–1,885; Yerwa Secrets ₦1,800–2,700. Maison Yusif's 100ml is $135 with no naira price. On ticket size Beguile's ₦76,000–86,000 is the lowest full-bottle price among the premium houses, because Jan Deux reaches a similar ticket only by selling 50ml.

**Gaps Beguile could own.**
- Free 3ml sampling plus a scent-finder quiz: no Nigerian rival in this set offers either.
- Verified-buyer reviews: Jan Deux's and Maison Yusif's testimonials are unverified or lightly evidenced, Yerwa and Toke Makinwa show none.
- 100ml as standard at under ₦90,000. BusinessDay (26 Apr 2026) reports that Nigerian niche buyers expect 100ml bottles and stronger scents; Jan Deux's 50ml format works against that.
- Clear delivery and returns terms. Only Yerwa spells out Nigerian delivery times, and its returns pages contradict each other.
- In-stock availability through December.

**Threats.**
- Jan Deux's growth rate, gifting packaging and Lekki shop target the same Lagos gift buyer at a higher, more "luxury" price, which can make Beguile look like the mid-tier choice.
- Perfumeology undercuts slightly at ₦80,000 and locks customers in with a discount membership.
- Majestic Roobee's 50.7K audience and ₦25,000 entry price capture first-time buyers before they trade up.
- Toke Makinwa's celebrity reach and Seinde Signature's shelves give it offline discovery Beguile lacks.
- Heritage-led brands (Yerwa, Maison Yusif) are winning the international press narrative about African fragrance, although Beguile was also named in the same BellaNaija feature.

## Recommended moves

1. **Hold the line on price and lead Black Friday with value, not depth.** With Beguile already at the bottom of the premium per-ml range, cap the "up to 40%" at a few scents or bundles and make the headline "100ml, in stock, delivered before Christmas" while Jan Deux and Toke Makinwa are sold out.
2. **Turn free samples into the main acquisition ad in Nigeria.** Run "try before you commit, free 3ml" creative against the ₦40,000–100,000 discovery sets rivals charge for, and route it through the quiz to capture emails.
3. **Launch a gifting layer for December.** Jan Deux sells wrapping at ₦5,000–10,000, a note scroll and a vanity bag; Toke Makinwa sells gift boxes at ₦217,500. A Beguile gift wrap, note card and a boxed 100ml plus oil set would lift basket size with little product development.
4. **Add a retention mechanic.** Perfumeology's ₦5,000/month Seduction Circle shows members will pay for a standing discount. A free points or refill-credit programme, or early access tiers built on the existing early-access list, would answer it without discounting to everyone.
5. **Get one physical touchpoint in Lagos and Abuja.** A stockist arrangement with a niche retailer or recurring pop-ups (the Abuja pop-up is already an Instagram highlight) would close the try-in-person gap against Jan Deux, Majestic Roobee and Toke Makinwa.
