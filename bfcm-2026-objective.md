## Walk-back (how the number was built)

1. **Stock sets the ceiling.** Lagos available minus 2.15 months reserve = sellable in window. Mystique 835, Sweet Oud 242, Mini Set 230, Goddess 86, Golden Kiss negative.
2. **Nigeria target = 70–85% of sellable** on Mystique, Sweet Oud, Mini Set. Goddess at run rate. 1,146 bottles at realised prices (₦78–81k) = ₦90M, plus Passion and Oil Trio = ₦100M.
3. **Orders = bottles ÷ 1.53 per order** = 750 NG orders, 58/day against 20/day baseline. Incremental 490 orders × ₦30k CPA cap = ₦15M budget.
4. **Abroad uplift sized to the offer**, not the stock. 25% off on two overstocked heroes plus list early access is good for 3–4x on a small base. Spend capped where contribution hits zero.
5. **Contribution** = net − 30% cost − gateway fees (Paystack ₦2k, Shopify Payments 4.5%) − ₦2–5k shipping subsidy − gift at cost (₦12k per oil) − ad budget.
