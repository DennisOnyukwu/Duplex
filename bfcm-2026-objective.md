# Beguile BFCM 2026 — The Objective

Window: Thu 20 Nov to Wed 2 Dec 2026 (13 days). All money in naira, net of discounts.
Data as of 14 Oct 2026: Shopify orders and inventory, Meta Ads, Klaviyo, GA4 via Windsor.

Stated assumptions (not facts): product cost = 30% of net price. No hero stock lands in Lagos before 2 Dec. December reserve held in Lagos = 2.15 months of normal demand (covers 14 Oct–19 Nov and 3–31 Dec).

---

## The objective

**Sell 1,700 full-price bottles, led by 870 Mystique. Clear 2,600 units of dead and overstocked inventory. Deliver ₦180M revenue in 13 days, 4.6x current run rate, at ₦62M contribution after ad spend.**

That is the stretch. The floor, built only from what the stock and margin comfortably allow, is ₦128M and ₦53M. Below the floor the event failed. Between floor and stretch is the job.

|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | ₦39M | ₦128M | **₦180M** |
| Orders | 322 | 950 | **1,425** |
| Full-price bottles | 484 | 1,440 | **1,700** |

| Ad spend | ₦9M | ₦24M | **₦37M** |
| Contribution after ads | ~₦18M | ₦53M | **₦62M** |

Baseline is the last 30 days scaled to 13 days. Last year's same window with no event did ₦35M.

Why the stretch is reachable: Mystique in Lagos has 835 units sellable after the December reserve and the floor only used 600. The dead stock has no ceiling. And the stretch does not touch hero price, so every extra Mystique, Sweet Oud or Mini Set sold is full margin.

## The offer per market

**Nigeria — heroes at full price, dead stock made irresistible.**
- Mystique, Sweet Oud, Mini Set, Goddess at full price. No codes, no % off. Ever.
- **A free 20ml oil in every order.** No threshold. Customer picks the scent at checkout.
- **The Oil Bar: all three 20ml oils for ₦45k.** List is ₦120k. That is the headline number that gets shared.
- **Passion at ₦45k** (from ₦70k). **Oil Trio at ₦60k** (from ₦100k).
- **Golden Hour: one oil at ₦10k, 60 minutes a day, announced that morning.** Scarcity and a daily reason to open the app.
- Golden Kiss pulled from paid once Lagos drops under 50 units.
- Paid: Meta ₦22M, pre-loaded from 10 Nov. Mystique, Sweet Oud, Mini Set campaigns plus one Oil Bar campaign. Hard CPA cap ₦30k.

**UK and Canada — "we over-ordered, you win."**
- **Hero Duo: Goddess + Golden Kiss together.** UK ₦150k (list ₦250k). Canada ₦120k (list ₦196k). 40% off, two bottles out the door per order.
- Goddess or Golden Kiss alone: 25% off.
- **Vault Box: Passion + Seduction or Blush Bloom + two oils.** UK ₦70k, Canada ₦60k. Over ₦230k of list in a box. Clears four dead units per order.
- Mystique full price, UK capped at 15, Canada at 35.
- Free 20ml oil in every order.
- Messaging angle is honest and new for the brand: the warehouse is full, the prices show it, this is not a trick.
- Email early access 18 Nov to the UK and Canada lists. Paid: retargeting plus cold allowed only under the ₦60k CPA cap.

**USA — full price, acquire hard.**
- No discounts. Free 3ml sample duo in every order (190 Mystique, 82 Goddess, 75 Golden Kiss at the 3PL).
- Free shipping over ₦150k.
- **Scent Passport referral:** buyer sends a friend a free sample set, friend's first order gives both a 10ml Golden Kiss.
- Paid ₦5M at ₦100k CPA cap. First order already profitable here. Scale while under cap.

## Walk-back (how the number was built)

1. **Stock sets the ceiling.** Lagos available minus 2.15 months reserve = sellable in window. Mystique 835, Sweet Oud 242, Mini Set 230, Goddess 86, Golden Kiss negative.
2. **Stretch Nigeria takes 95% of sellable** on Mystique, Sweet Oud, Mini Set. The floor took 70–85%. Goddess at run rate. 1,416 bottles at realised prices (₦78–81k) = ₦111M.
3. **Dead stock priced to move, not to protect.** Oil Bar at ₦45k clears ₦36k of product cost and leaves ₦9k. Passion at ₦45k leaves ₦24k. Every unit sold is cash out of a warehouse that was never going to release it. Adds ₦25M in Nigeria.
4. **Orders = bottles ÷ 1.53 per order** plus dead-stock-only orders = 1,050 NG orders, 81/day against 20/day. Incremental 790 orders × ₦30k cap = ₦22M budget.
5. **Abroad uplift sized to the offer.** A 40% Hero Duo and a four-unit Vault Box are strong enough to bring the UK back to its July run rate. Spend capped where contribution hits zero.
6. **Contribution** = net − 30% cost − gateway fees (Paystack ₦2k, Shopify Payments 4.5%) − ₦2–5k shipping subsidy − free oil at cost (₦12k each) − ad budget. The stretch clears 2,600 overstock units and still adds ₦9M contribution over the floor, because the Nigeria heroes carry it.
