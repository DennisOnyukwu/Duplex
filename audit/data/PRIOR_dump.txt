15: Start here: what the audit found
16: Over 12 months the account spent ₦189.2M for 9,607 Meta-attributed purchases worth ₦1.19B (ROAS 6.28, CPA ₦19,695). The money is not the problem. The structure is.
17: • The account never leaves the learning phase. 476 campaigns were launched in 12 months, with a median life of 8 days. Of 2,742 ad-set-weeks, none reached 50 purchases.
18: • Proven winners get paused. 27 Sales ad sets that returned at least 9× are now paused. Examples: CEO Passion NG at 22.45, Passion & Seduction at 15.26, NG Catalogue at 12.05. Each was replaced by a fresh ad set that starts learning from zero.
19: • International spend is the largest drag. The UK, Canada and the US took 48.9% of spend (₦92.6M) at ROAS 3.58 and CPA ₦38,875. Nigeria returned 8.86 at CPA ₦13,364. Of the 149 Sales-objective ad sets that spent past the threshold with zero purchases, 145 were international.
20: Single primary bottleneck: fragmentation. Budget is spread across dozens of short-lived ad sets on ₦10,000–₦15,000 a day, none of which can gather the roughly 50 purchases a week that Meta needs.
21: Most critical recommendation: consolidate Nigeria into one or two long-running Sales campaigns, with a budget that can clear 50 purchases a week (about ₦95,000 a day per ad set at Nigeria's observed CPA). Feed new creative in as ads inside those campaigns, not as new campaigns.
22: Treat every efficiency figure below as an upper bound. In September 2026, Meta reported more purchases (861) than Shopify recorded in total (804).
