# Brand profile: Beguile

The business every competitor is measured against. Edit this file when the brand changes.

- **Site:** https://beguile.com (Shopify; prices listed in NGN)
- **Instagram:** https://www.instagram.com/beguilebyomaa
- **What it is:** "Modern Fragrance House". Nigerian-founded fine fragrance brand, started in 2021 by Ihuoma Eze. Compositions developed with perfumers in Dubai.
- **Positioning:** scent as identity; long-lasting performance; each scent named for a feeling, not a note.
- **Range:** seven 100ml perfumes (Mystique, Goddess, Golden Kiss, Blush Bloom, Sweet Oud, Passion, Seduction), 20ml perfume oils (Clean Musk, Vanilla Sugar, White Florals), a Mini Set, 10ml travel size, 3ml samples, merch (totes, bottle).
- **Prices (Nov 2026):** 100ml perfume ₦76,000–86,000; 20ml oil ₦38,000; 10ml ₦22,000; Mini Set ₦86,000.
- **Bestsellers:** Mystique, Goddess.
- **Sales levers seen on site:** scent-finder quiz, verified-buyer reviews, early-access list for Black Friday (up to 40% off), free 3ml samples.
- **Ships to:** Nigeria, USA, UK, Canada (shipping policy lists these four).
- **Customer:** largely women; Nigerian and diaspora buyers.

## What counts as a competitor

A brand a Beguile customer in that country could plausibly buy instead:

1. Independent or niche fragrance houses selling direct online, in a similar price band for that market.
2. African-founded or diaspora-led fragrance brands selling into that country.
3. Brands with the same scent territory (musk, oud, vanilla, gourmand, florals) and "long-lasting" promise, including perfume-oil brands.

Not competitors: multi-brand retailers and marketplaces, mass designer houses (Dior, Chanel), dupe/clone sellers unless they are clearly taking Beguile's customers in that market.
