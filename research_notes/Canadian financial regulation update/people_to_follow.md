Compiled 2026-11-26 for a Regulatory Advisory Manager at a federal credit union (Coast Capital). Excludes regulator heads and office-holders (covered in `sources_to_follow.md`).

## How to read this

- **Evidence strength** uses three labels: `verified recent activity (2026)`, `verified but older`, `role verified, activity not verified`.
- "Verified" means the byline, role or link was seen on a page fetched or in a search result during this session. Nothing here comes from LinkedIn or Instagram directly; both block fetching.
- A LinkedIn URL is given only where it appeared on a fetched page (a firm bio/bulletin or the Open Banking Expo Powerlist). Otherwise it reads "LinkedIn URL not verified".
- No individual's LinkedIn posting frequency was checked. Firm bulletins, blogs, newsletters and podcasts are the verified channels.
- Channel reality: almost everyone here is on firm publications and (presumably) LinkedIn. Three have their own newsletter/blog (Davis, Scassa, McLister), one has a podcast (Sivan). **No credible Instagram or TikTok creator explaining Canadian bank regulation to a professional audience was found.** YouTube presence is organizational (firm and association channels), not personal.
