# Sources to follow: Canadian financial services regulation (for a Regulatory Advisory Manager at Coast Capital, as of 2026-11-26)

**How things were verified (read first).** Checks were run on 2026-11-25/26.
- "Linked from official site" = the handle/URL was found as a link on the organization's own website.
- YouTube "last video" dates come from each channel's public YouTube RSS feed (`youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=...`).
- Podcast "last episode" dates come from the Apple Podcasts (Canada store) directory lookup; the date is Apple's `releaseDate` for the most recent episode.
- Website/feed "last item" dates come from the site's home page or RSS feed.
- LinkedIn and Instagram block automated access, so **no LinkedIn or Instagram posting dates could be checked for anyone**. For those two channels, existence is confirmed only where the official website links to the profile. Personal LinkedIn profile URLs were not verified except where stated.
- Anything marked **UNVERIFIED** or **DORMANT** should be treated accordingly.

## 1. Regulators and public bodies: official channels, feeds and email alerts

### Takeaway
Every core regulator has a verifiable website news feed, and most have YouTube, LinkedIn and X; the highest-value "push" services are OSFI email notifications, the FINTRAC mailing list, Bank of Canada email alerts/RSS, Canada Gazette RSS and the LEGISinfo custom bill RSS feed. Instagram exists only for the consumer-facing bodies (Bank of Canada, FCAC, CDIC, Finance Canada, BCSC); OSFI, FINTRAC, OBSI, the Privacy Commissioner, the Competition Bureau and BCFSA do not list an Instagram account on their sites.

### Inferences (the proposed routine)
**Every morning (RSS reader or inbox rules):**
1. OSFI RSS / email notifications
2. FINTRAC RSS + mailing list
3. FCAC news feed
4. Bank of Canada email alerts (press releases, speeches)
5. Finance Canada news releases (consultations, draft regulations)
6. Canada Gazette Part I (Saturdays) and Part II (alternate Wednesdays) RSS — check on publication days
7. CDIC, OBSI and OPC news feeds (low volume)
8. The Logic + Globe ROB / Financial Post banking headlines

**Weekly (30-45 minutes, e.g., Friday):**
- LinkedIn sweep of the regulator company pages and the named office-holders
- Law-firm insights: Osler, McCarthy Tétrault, Torys, Blakes, Fasken (via email subscriptions) and a Mondaq/Lexology scan
- LEGISinfo custom RSS for bills touching the Bank Act, PCMLTFA, privacy and consumer-driven banking; Senate BANC and House FINA meeting notices
- BCFSA advisories and news; CCUA Insider; Payments Canada news
- Canadian Mortgage Trends and BetaKit for mortgage and fintech/open-banking developments
- One podcast: Torys or Stikeman, plus an AML or privacy show as time allows

**Monthly:**
- BLG Federal Financial Institutions Legislative and Regulatory Reporter as a completeness check against your own log
- Outlier Compliance blog; C.D. Howe and GRI publications

**Quarterly / annual calendar:**
- OSFI quarterly release days and the Annual Risk Outlook (with its semi-annual update)
- Bank of Canada rate decisions and Monetary Policy Reports; Financial Stability Report
- Federal budget (autumn in 2025 and 2026) and spring/fall economic updates
- FINTRAC and FCAC annual reports; OBSI annual report; OPC annual report to Parliament
- GRI Summit (October), Payments Canada SUMMIT (May), Open Banking Expo Canada, year-end law-firm outlooks (Osler Legal Outlook, typically early December)
