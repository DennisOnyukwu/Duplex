# OSFI prudential guidelines and rules, Nov 2023 – Nov 2026 (federal credit union / SMSB lens)

Research date: 2026-11-26. "SMSB" = small and medium-sized bank (OSFI's term; includes federal credit unions). "FRFI" = federally regulated financial institution. Dates of the form "Nov 1 / Jan 1" mean Nov 1 for October 31 fiscal year-ends and Jan 1 for December 31 fiscal year-ends (a December year-end institution uses the January date). Where a fact came only from a search-result summary rather than a page I opened, it is marked "(search summary)".

## 1. Non-financial risk guidelines: B-10, B-13, B-15, E-21, E-23, Integrity and Security, Culture, E-13

### Takeaway
The big operational-risk build-out of 2023–2024 is now fully in force (B-10 since May 1, 2024; Integrity and Security since Jan 31 / Jul 31, 2025; E-21 full adherence Sept 1, 2026), and the one large item still ahead is Guideline E-23 Model Risk Management (covers AI/ML), effective May 1, 2027. Climate guideline B-15 was loosened: Scope 3 disclosure pushed to fiscal 2028 and the off-balance-sheet AUM financed-emissions expectation deferred with no date; the Culture and Behaviour Risk draft was never finalized and was replaced by a short Regulatory Notice.

### Cited Findings

**B-10 Third-Party Risk Management**
- Final B-10 gave institutions a transition period to self-assess and build adherence by May 1, 2024; new arrangements were expected to comply from that date (search summary of Torys) — [Torys](https://www.torys.com/en/our-latest-thinking/publications/2023/05/osfi-b-10-third-party-risk-management)
- OSFI later made consequential amendments to B-10 and B-13 for foreign bank branches, giving branches until March 31, 2025 to adhere to B-10 (not applicable to a federal credit union, but shows the guideline was revised) (search summary) — [OSFI](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/consequential-amendments-guidelines-b-10-b-13-related-foreign-branches)
- NEW, Nov 19, 2026: targeted amendment to B-10 for consumer-driven banking (open banking), effective immediately. Within the federal framework under the Consumer-Driven Banking Act, institutions should rely on Government of Canada accreditation of participating entities rather than performing the B-10 risk assessments and contractual arrangements; institutions "will not be expected to duplicate the federal accreditation process" but remain responsible for residual operational-resilience risks including data integrity and cyber — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-targeted-amendment-guideline-b-10-consumer-driven-banking)

**B-13 Technology and Cyber Risk Management**
- OSFI's technology and cyber risk page (last modified Oct 15, 2026) still presents B-13 as the governing guideline, to be read "from a risk-based perspective"; the page also says OSFI endorses the Financial Stability Board's sound practices for responsible AI adoption — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/risks/technology-cyber-risk-management)

**B-15 Climate Risk Management**
- Revised B-15 published March 7, 2025 to align with the final Canadian Sustainability Standards Board (CSSB) standards; Scope 3 GHG disclosure for all FRFIs postponed from fiscal year-end 2025 to fiscal 2028 (search summary) — [OSFI letter](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/letter-industry-we-are-updating-guideline-b-15-final-cssb-standards); [McCarthy Tétrault](https://www.mccarthy.ca/en/insights/articles/osfi-releases-updated-guideline-climate-risk-management)
- The 2025 revision set fiscal 2029 for Scope 3 emissions on off-balance-sheet assets under management (search summary) — [McCarthy Tétrault](https://www.mccarthy.ca/en/insights/articles/osfi-releases-updated-guideline-climate-risk-management)
- Jan 8, 2026: OSFI paused the planned public consultation on the financed-emissions disclosure expectation for off-balance-sheet AUM; Jan 29, 2026: implementation of that expectation deferred "to a future date" (reflected in Annex 2-2 of B-15), no new date given — [OSFI Q1 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-continuing-advance-smart-well-calibrated-risk-taking); [OSFI deferral notice](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/deferral-public-consultation-guideline-b-15-disclosure-expectation-financed-emissions-related)
- Nov 19, 2026: OSFI published insights from the 2026 Climate Risk Returns and updated returns — [OSFI Q4 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfi-launches-its-competitive-prudential-initiative-putting-resilience-work-support-greater)

**E-21 Operational Risk Management and Resilience**
- Final E-21 published Aug 22, 2024. Sections 1 and 2 (governance, operational risk management) applied immediately; remaining expectations phased, with full adherence and operationalization expected by Sept 1, 2026 (search summary) — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-guideline-e-21-operational-risk-resilience); [OSFI letter](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/operational-risk-management-resilience-letter)
- KPMG's timeline: adherence to Section 4 by Sept 1, 2025; full adherence Sept 1, 2026; scenario testing of all critical operations complete by Sept 1, 2027 (search summary; the 2027 date was not reconciled against OSFI's text) — [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ca/en/services/advisory/risk-services/operational-resilience.html)

**E-23 Model Risk Management (includes AI/ML)**
- Final E-23 published Sept 11, 2025; effective May 1, 2027. Applies to all FRFIs (pension plans excluded). Principles-based, covers all models "regardless of the technology used", applied in a risk-based, proportional way. OSFI rejected a requested three-year implementation period and did not add a grace period for third-party models; third-party models are to be handled under B-10 principles (search summary) — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-guideline-e-23-model-risk-management); [OSFI letter](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/guideline-e-23-model-risk-management-2027-letter); [Blakes](https://www.blakes.com/insights/osfi-releases-final-guideline-e-23-for-model-risk-management-and-ai-use-by-frfis/)

**Integrity and Security Guideline**
- Published Jan 31, 2024; in effect Jan 31, 2025, except the new background-check expectations, effective July 31, 2025 (search summary) — [Fasken](https://www.fasken.com/en/knowledge/2024/02/final-osfi-integrity-and-security-guideline-released); [OSFI letter](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/integrity-security-letter)
- May 21, 2026: OSFI updated its Guide to Intervention for Federally Regulated Deposit-Taking Institutions to reflect the expanded integrity and security mandate, its risk appetite and the Supervisory Framework — [OSFI Q2 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-strengthening-resilience-while-reducing-complexity)

**Culture and Behaviour Risk**
- The draft Culture and Behaviour Risk Guideline (issued Feb 28, 2023; consultation to May 31, 2023) was never finalized. OSFI issued a Regulatory Notice on Culture Risk Management, effective immediately, which "replaces and supersedes" the draft; it focuses on governance of culture risk, fostering a desired culture and enterprise-wide culture management (search summary) — [OSFI letter](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/regulatory-notice-culture-risk-management-letter); [OSFI backgrounder](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-culture-risk-management-regulatory-notice)
- Fasken dates the Notice to November 2024 and noted updated guidance was then expected in 2025 (search summary) — [Fasken](https://fasken.com/en/knowledge/2024/12/banking-regulatory-year-in-review-and-2025-outlook)

**E-13 Regulatory Compliance Management**
- The most recent version found is the 2014 revision; no 2025–2026 revision, draft or rescission was found, and OSFI's Integrity and Security Guideline still cross-references E-13 (search summary) — [OSFI E-13 letter (2014)](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/revised-guideline-e-13-regulatory-compliance-management-rcm-letter-2014)

**Governance / accountability (related)**
- Jan 29, 2026: OSFI launched a nine-month consultation on governance and accountability for boards and senior leaders; no closing date or effective date stated on the release page — [OSFI Q1 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-continuing-advance-smart-well-calibrated-risk-taking)
- In its Nov 21, 2024 policy review OSFI said a new foundational risk management guideline and a revised Corporate Governance Guideline were planned for the year ahead — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-results-osfis-policy-review)

### Inferences
- For a federal credit union the live compliance deadline is E-23 on May 1, 2027: a model inventory, risk rating and lifecycle governance covering vendor and AI/ML models must be in place by then, with no third-party grace period.
- E-21 has passed its Sept 1, 2026 full-adherence date, so supervisory work will now test operation (critical operations, impact tolerances, scenario testing) rather than design.
- The Nov 19, 2026 B-10 amendment removes a conflict between third-party due diligence and mandatory data sharing under consumer-driven banking; compliance teams should update third-party risk procedures to carve out accredited participants while keeping residual-risk controls.
- The nine-month governance consultation launched Jan 29, 2026 would have closed around late October 2026 (arithmetic from the stated length), so a draft governance/accountability guideline in 2027 is plausible but not announced.

### Gaps
- B-13's effective date was not confirmed on a page opened in this session. My background knowledge is that B-13 was finalized July 2022 and took effect January 1, 2024, but this is unverified here.
- B-10's original publication date (April 2023, from background knowledge) was not verified in session.
- Whether OSFI issued the "updated culture guidance expected in 2025" is unknown; none was found.
- Status and any output of the governance/accountability consultation after Jan 2026 not found.
- E-21: the Sept 1, 2027 scenario-testing date (KPMG) versus the Sept 1, 2026 full-adherence date should be checked against the guideline text.
- No evidence found of any change to E-13; absence in searches is not proof of no change.

## 2. Capital and liquidity: CAR, output floor, SMSB framework, DSB, LAR, crypto, Pillar 3, large exposures

### Takeaway
The most consequential item for a federal credit union is OSFI's Nov 19, 2026 "Competitive Prudential Initiative": a draft CAR Guideline (2028) that cuts standardized risk weights for SMSBs and would exempt SMSBs, including federal credit unions, from the Tier 1 minimum/target and NVCC requirement — consultation closes Feb 17, 2027, proposed effective Nov 1, 2027 / Jan 1, 2028. Separately, the Basel III output floor has been frozen at 67.5% since Feb 12, 2025 "until further notice", the Domestic Stability Buffer was cut to 3.0% on June 19, 2026, and final CAR (2027) and crypto-asset guidelines take effect Nov 1, 2026 / Jan 1, 2027.

### Cited Findings

**Basel III output floor**
- OSFI first delayed the increase of the output floor by one year (2024 announcement) — [OSFI](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfi-delays-increase-basel-iii-output-floor-one-year)
- Feb 12, 2025: OSFI deferred further increases "until further notice"; the floor stays at 67.5%, and OSFI committed to give affected banks at least two years' notice before resuming increases (search summary) — [OSFI statement](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/statement-superintendent-financial-institutions-basel-iii-standardized-capital-floor-level); [BNN Bloomberg](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/business/2025/02/13/canadian-regulator-defers-basel-capital-floor-hikes-indefinitely)
- The final CAR Guideline (2026) restates that increases to the Basel III standardized capital floor are deferred until further notice — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-final-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2026)

**CAR Guideline (2026)** — final Sept 11, 2025 (consultation Feb 20 – Apr 22, 2025); effective Nov 1, 2025 / Jan 1, 2026
- Changes: revised treatment of certain U.S. government-sponsored entities; implementation considerations for combined loan plans (CLPs — multiple lending products secured by the same property), with 18 months for banks to change internal models; market risk rules aligned with credit risk treatment of sovereign exposures; clarification of mortgage classification for capital purposes (expectations in CAR since 2023; affects capital calculation, not underwriting); confirmation that rental income can still be used in underwriting under B-20; corresponding revisions to the SMSB Capital and Liquidity Requirements Guideline (details not specified) — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-final-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2026)
- OSFI also published a clarification on rental income and mortgage classification — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/risks/real-estate-secured-lending/clarifying-osfis-guidance-rental-income-mortgage-classification)

**CAR Guideline (2027)** — final Sept 10, 2026 (90-day consultation launched Nov 20, 2025); effective Nov 1, 2026 / Jan 1, 2027
- Changes in credit risk, securitization and market risk; a streamlined application and approval process for SMSBs seeking the internal ratings-based approach; changes to SME lending and to land acquisition, development and construction financing intended to support lending to smaller corporates and construction; consolidation of implementation notes — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-final-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2027)

**Draft CAR Guideline (2028) — "Competitive Prudential Initiative"** — published Nov 19, 2026; comments due Feb 17, 2027; proposed effective Nov 1, 2027 / Jan 1, 2028
- OSFI: "OSFI will lower capital intensity for lending by small and medium-sized banks (SMSBs)" in residential mortgages, SME lending and corporate lending; described as "our most ambitious effort to date to level the playing field in Canadian banking" — [OSFI news release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfi-launches-its-competitive-prudential-initiative-putting-resilience-work-support-greater)
- SMSB-only preferential standardized risk weights for general residential real estate where the borrower's credit bureau score is above 720 (income-producing residential real estate excluded). 2027 → draft 2028 by LTV: <50% 20% → 10%; 50–60% 25% → 12.5%; 60–70% 30% → 20%; 70–80% 35% → 27.5%; 80–90% 40% → 35%; 90–100% 50% → 45%; >100% 70% → 65% — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-draft-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2028)
- Standardized corporate risk weights: SME corporates 75% → 65%; flat corporate 100% → 80%; unrated investment-grade corporates 65% → 60% — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-draft-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2028)
- Capital structure for SMSBs including federal credit unions: remove the minimum and supervisory target Tier 1 risk-based capital requirement; exempt from the non-viability contingent capital (NVCC) requirement for Additional Tier 1 and Tier 2 instruments — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-draft-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2028)
- Credit risk mitigation: third-party GICs recognized as collateral; net cash surrender value of life insurance treated as a guarantee by the insurer (both subject to operational criteria). Government-sponsored venture capital fund investments: 400% → 100% risk weight — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-draft-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2028)
- Tightening: mortgages with LTV above 65% that could become negatively amortizing would carry 1.75 times the general residential risk weight under both IRB and standardized approaches (the backgrounder's worked example, 30% → 55%, does not match 1.75 × 30% = 52.5%) — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-draft-capital-adequacy-requirements-guideline-2028)
- Same release: covered bond limit revisions to give SMSBs and D-SIBs more stable funding access; changes to market risk capital requirements (no detail on the release page) — [OSFI news release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfi-launches-its-competitive-prudential-initiative-putting-resilience-work-support-greater)

**Domestic Stability Buffer (DSB)**
- Held at 3.5% in December 2025; lowered to 3.0% effective June 19, 2026 (first change since June 2023), and the DSB range lowered to 0–3% from 0–4%. Total supervisory CET1 expectation for D-SIBs becomes 11% (4.5% minimum + 2.5% conservation buffer + 1% surcharge + 3% DSB) (search summary) — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/osfi-lowers-domestic-stability-buffer-30-so-canadas-largest-banks-can-deploy-more-capital); [BNN Bloomberg](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/2026/06/19/federal-banking-regulator-osfi-lowers-domestic-stability-buffer-to-3/)

**Liquidity (LAR, ILAAP, B-6)**

- May 21, 2026: draft LAR Guideline (2027) and a draft Internal Liquidity Adequacy Assessment Process (ILAAP) Guideline (structure for assessing, managing and reporting liquidity risk, scaled to size and complexity) — [OSFI Q2 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-strengthening-resilience-while-reducing-complexity)

**Crypto-asset exposures**
- Final Capital and Liquidity Treatment of Crypto-asset Exposures (Banking) Guideline (2027) published Sept 10, 2026 (draft update May 21, 2026); effective Nov 1, 2026 / Jan 1, 2027. Two targeted changes after consultation: recognition of certain hedging across regulated exchanges for Group 2a crypto-assets, and exclusion of qualifying client-clearing activity from the exposure limit (search summary) — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-capital-liquidity-treatment-crypto-asset-exposures-banking-guideline-2027-1)

### Inferences
- The output floor and the DSB bind only IRB banks and D-SIBs respectively; a standardized-approach federal credit union is not directly affected, but both were the source of the competitive gap that the draft CAR (2028) now tries to narrow from the SMSB side.
- If finalized as drafted, CAR (2028) is materially favourable to a federal credit union: lower residential and commercial risk weights and removal of the Tier 1 target and NVCC requirement (the last two were long-standing credit-union asks because member shares and retained earnings dominate their capital). The under-720 credit score cut-off and the IPRRE exclusion mean data on bureau scores and property-income dependence will drive how much benefit is realized.
- The 1.75x multiplier for potentially negatively amortizing mortgages is the one tightening measure and targets variable-rate fixed-payment products.
- A December 31 year-end federal credit union is, as of today, five weeks from CAR (2027) and the crypto guideline taking effect (Jan 1, 2027).

- The date of the first one-year output-floor delay (background knowledge: July 2024) was not confirmed on an opened page.
- Specific revisions to the SMSB Capital and Liquidity Requirements Guideline in 2025–2026, and SMSB category thresholds, were not found; whether the draft CAR (2028) is accompanied by a revised SMSB Guideline is unknown.
- Covered bond limit numbers and the market risk changes in the Nov 19, 2026 release were not on the pages opened.
- Whether the draft B-2 for SMSBs takes effect Nov 2027 / Jan 2028 (May 2026 backgrounder) or Q1 2029 (Nov 2026 release) is unresolved; OSFI's own pages conflict on the final-publication date.
- The December 2026 DSB decision had not been found as of 2026-11-26.

- Details of LAR (2026) changes beyond "retail deposit categories, effective May 1, 2026" not retrieved.

## 3. Real estate lending: B-20, LTI limits, MQR / stress test, straight switches, combined loan plans

### Takeaway
Portfolio loan-to-income (LTI) limits (institution-specific cap on the share of new uninsured mortgages above 4.5x income) have been in effect since each institution's fiscal 2025 start and were confirmed as permanent-in-place on Jan 29, 2026; the minimum qualifying rate (greater of contract rate + 2% or 5.25%) is unchanged, but has not applied to uninsured straight switches since Nov 21, 2024. B-20 itself is slated to be folded into a single principles-based Credit Risk Management Guideline, with draft chapters now deferred to Feb 12, 2027.

### Cited Findings
- LTI limit: a supervisory measure on the volume of newly originated uninsured mortgages above a 4.5x loan-to-income multiple; each institution has its own limit; it "is not a limit on each individual loan". In effect since each institution's 2025 fiscal year start; quarterly reporting to OSFI. Exclusions: insured loans, reverse mortgages, renewals, and refinances where the only change is extended contractual amortization. "Loan" includes all loans secured on the property (first and second mortgages, HELOCs, at any lender); income uses the institution's own definition consistent with its debt-service calculations (page last modified Jan 29, 2026) — [OSFI](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/supervision/financial-institutions/banks/loan-income-limits-uninsured-mortgage-portfolios)
- Limits are set considering each institution's historical LTI proportions in low- and high-rate periods plus housing and economic vulnerability indicators — [OSFI](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/supervision/financial-institutions/banks/loan-income-limits-uninsured-mortgage-portfolios)
- Jan 29, 2026: LTI limits remain in place following the pilot; "existing debt service expectations in Guideline B-20 will continue to complement LTI limits" — [OSFI Q1 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-continuing-advance-smart-well-calibrated-risk-taking)
- MQR for uninsured mortgages remains "the greater of the mortgage contract rate plus 2% or 5.25%", reviewed at least annually in consultation with the Department of Finance and Bank of Canada (page last modified Jan 29, 2026) — [OSFI MQR page](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/Eng/fi-if/in-ai/Pages/mqr-nfo.aspx)
- Trade press reported OSFI left the stress test unchanged in its first 2026 release (search summary) — [Mortgage Professional America](https://www.mpamag.com/ca/mortgage-industry/industry-trends/osfi-reveals-latest-decision-on-mortgage-stress-test/563642)
- Straight switches: announced September 2024, effective Nov 21, 2024 — OSFI "will no longer prescribe the minimum qualifying rate" for uninsured straight switches at renewal (move to another federally regulated lender with no increase in amortization or loan amount); the receiving lender must still underwrite the loan like any new origination (search summary of OSFI's letter and press) — [OSFI MQR backgrounder](https://osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-minimum-qualifying-rate-mqr); [BNN Bloomberg](https://bnnbloomberg.ca/business/real-estate/2024/11/21/mortgage-stress-test-changes-to-increase-lender-competition-real-estate-experts); [Ratehub](https://www.ratehub.ca/blog/breaking-news-osfi-drops-stress-test-requirement-for-mortgage-renewal-switches/)

### Inferences
- B-20 remains the operative underwriting guideline in late 2026; its replacement by the CRM Guideline is at least a 2027–2028 event (draft chapters only in Feb 2027), so the MQR's long-term fate will likely be decided inside that process.

## 4. Structural changes: Supervisory Framework, mandate, cyber testing and incident reporting, release-day approach, burden reduction, leadership

### Takeaway
OSFI changed how it supervises (new Supervisory Framework with an 8-point Overall Risk Rating from April 1, 2024), how it publishes (quarterly release days since 2024, with Industry Day two weeks later) and, since 2025, what it is optimizing for: a visible pivot toward regulatory efficiency, competition and SMSB relief, capped by Budget 2026's proposal to give OSFI a statutory secondary competition objective and to let provincial credit unions acquire federal ones. Enforcement is also changing: a Guide to Administrative Monetary Penalties now applies to violations after Sept 11, 2025, and OSFI says it will use AMPs more often.

### Cited Findings

**Supervisory Framework**
- New Supervisory Framework effective April 1, 2024, replacing the prior approach with an eight-point Overall Risk Rating (1 = minimal risk, 8 = non-viability imminent), a five-point Tier Rating based on size, complexity and contagion potential, and (for Tiers 1–4) ratings in four risk categories; intervention stages continue on the existing scale (search summary) — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/progress-our-initiatives/supervisory-approach-fit-future/osfis-new-supervisory-framework); [Torys](https://www.torys.com/en/our-latest-thinking/publications/2024/02/osfis-new-supervisory-approach-to-financial-institutions)
- Goodmans gave April 4, 2024 as the expected date, which conflicts with OSFI's April 1 (search summary) — [Goodmans](https://goodmans.ca/insights/article/osfi-releases-new-framework-to-modernize-financial-supervision)
- Guide to Intervention for deposit-taking institutions updated May 21, 2026 for the integrity and security mandate — [OSFI Q2 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-strengthening-resilience-while-reducing-complexity)

**Cyber: incident reporting and I-CRT**
- Technology and Cyber Security Incident Reporting Advisory (effective Aug 13, 2021): report to OSFI's Technology Risk Division and the Lead Supervisor within 24 hours or sooner; as of April 1, 2025 incidents are to be submitted on a new form (search summary) — [OSFI advisory](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/Eng/fi-if/rg-ro/gdn-ort/adv-prv/Pages/TCSIR.aspx); [OSFI instructions](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/osfi-technology-cyber-incident-report-detailed-instructions)
- Intelligence-led Cyber Resilience Testing (I-CRT) framework released April 21, 2023; applies to systemically important banks and internationally active insurance groups (search summary) — [OSFI I-CRT](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/osfis-intelligence-led-cyber-resilience-testing-crt-framework)

**Release-day approach**
- "Since August 2024" OSFI releases guidance through Quarterly Releases followed two weeks later by Industry Day. 2026 release days: Jan 29, May 21, Sept 10, Nov 19 (Industry Day Dec 3, 2026). Next: Feb 12, 2027 (Industry Day Feb 26, 2027) with final LAR (2027), draft B-6, final B-2 [DTI], draft CRM chapters — [OSFI Q3 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-resilience-enables-competition-innovation-sustainable-economic-growth-0); [OSFI schedule](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/progress-our-initiatives/modernizing-policies-guidance-supervision-regulatory-efficiency/osfis-policy-releases-announcements-schedule)

**Regulatory burden reduction and rescissions**
- Nov 21, 2024 policy review: OSFI rescinding 20 guidelines and advisories that are "outdated, redundant, or no longer fit-for-purpose", initial batch effective April 1, 2025; the review "will become an ongoing process" — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-results-osfis-policy-review)
- Further rescissions dated Dec 31, 2025 include NSFR Disclosure Requirements (2019) and the 2005 reputation-risk letter (search summary) — [OSFI rescinded guidance](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/rescinded-guidance); additional removals from the Guidance Library — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-additional-rescission-or-removal-documents-osfis-guidance-library); Superintendent statement on continued regulatory efficiency — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/statement-superintendent-osfis-continued-regulatory-efficiency)
- Sept 3, 2026 policy-plan letter deferred draft B-6, final Pillar 3 IRRBB and draft CRM chapters — [OSFI letter](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/letter-industry-updates-osfis-policy-plan)

**New entry regime (relevant to credit union continuance)**
- Announced Feb 12, 2026 (previewed by the Superintendent Jan 30, 2026): fast-track entry framework for fintechs and provincial credit unions, pilot planned June 2026, public application dashboard, no statutory change. Targets: four weeks for phase-one feedback; recommendation to the Minister within 12 months "where dependencies permit"; about three months for the order to commence and carry on business. OSFI is moving away from a "no bank failure" stance and expects credible exit plans. Torys noted no tailored capital relief for credit unions was announced at that time — [Torys](https://www.torys.com/en/our-latest-thinking/publications/2026/02/osfi-to-fast-track-new-entry-regime-for-fintechs-and-credit-unions)

**Administrative monetary penalties**
- OSFI Guide to Administrative Monetary Penalties (Torys article dated Feb 5, 2026), following a September 2025 letter: applies to violations after Sept 11, 2025; earlier contraventions identified on or before Dec 31, 2026 stay under the old approach; excludes late/erroneous filing penalties. OSFI will impose AMPs more often, including for lower-level contraventions, and at higher amounts within statutory maximums ($500,000 entity / $100,000 individual for very serious violations); five-year lookback; OSFI will not publish names; institutions are asked to promptly disclose potential compliance issues to the Lead Supervisor — [Torys](https://www.torys.com/our-latest-thinking/publications/2026/02/osfi-releases-guide-on-administrative-monetary-penalties)

**Budget 2026 (Nov 5, 2026) and mandate**
- Budget 2026 proposes amending the OSFI Act to add a secondary competition objective so that "competition considerations, such as proportionality, compliance burden, and market impacts" are reflected in supervision; Bank Act changes to let qualifying cooperative shareholders invest in and control federal credit unions (with ministerial approval) and to let provincial credit unions acquire federal credit unions; OSFI asked to strengthen its AMP regime (search summary; proposals, not enacted law) — [McCarthy Tétrault](https://www.mccarthy.ca/en/insights/blogs/techlex/budget-2026-financial-institutions-update); [Fasken](https://www.fasken.com/en/knowledge/2026/11/2026-federal-budget-highlights-affecting-financial-services); [Torys](https://www.torys.com/about-us/news-and-media/2026/11/ottawa-signals-it-will-accept-more-risk-for-more-bank-competition)
- OSFI's Nov 19, 2026 release says its Competitive Prudential Initiative aligns with Budget 2026, which "proposes amending the OSFI Act to add a secondary competition mandate" — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfi-launches-its-competitive-prudential-initiative-putting-resilience-work-support-greater)
- Budget 2025 (November 2025) proposals for small and mid-sized federal financial institutions — [Torys](https://www.torys.com/en/our-latest-thinking/publications/2025/11/budget-2025-proposals-for-small-and-midsized-federal-financial-institutions)

**Leadership**
- Peter Routledge was still Superintendent as of a June 16, 2026 letter. 2026 changes: Ben Gully (Deputy Superintendent, Supervision) left to become Secretary General of the Basel Committee from August 2026; Angie Radiskovic took over Supervision effective June 10, 2026; Jing Yang joined as Deputy Superintendent, Risk, Strategy and Policy; Josée Turcotte appointed Deputy Superintendent, Integrity, National Security and Integrated Solutions (search summary) — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/changes-osfis-executive-leadership-team); [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/ben-gully-appointed-next-secretary-general-basel-committee-banking-supervision)

**Risk outlook**
- ARO 2026-27 top risks in order: real estate secured lending/mortgage; non-bank financial institution exposure (reintroduced); funding and liquidity; "other risks", where integrity and security (the prior year's top risk) now sits. The semi-annual update flags heightened geopolitical tension and AI-enabled cyber-attacks, including "autonomous capabilities" (search summary) — [OSFI ARO 2026-27](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/reports-publications/osfis-annual-risk-outlook-fiscal-year-2026-2027); [OSFI semi-annual update](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/reports-publications/annual-risk-outlook-semi-annual-update-fiscal-year-2026-2027)

### Inferences
- There is no new Superintendent: no source found shows a change, and Routledge was in office in mid-2026 and quoted in later releases. The question's premise of a "new Superintendent" appears not to hold as of the evidence found; the changes were at the Deputy level.
- The AMP guide plus the ask to self-disclose "potential" compliance issues is a direct change to a compliance function's escalation practice: delay in disclosure is now an aggravating factor.
- The Dec 31, 2026 cut-off for legacy contraventions (pre-Sept 11, 2025) to be handled under the old AMP approach is a near-term date for any look-back remediation.
- I-CRT is not directly applicable to an SMSB, and the output floor, DSB, TLAC and EDTF items are D-SIB matters; the items that do bind a federal credit union structurally are the Supervisory Framework ratings, 24-hour incident reporting, AMPs and the release-day cadence.

### Gaps
- Bill C-47 (Budget Implementation Act, 2023, No. 1) expanding OSFI's mandate to integrity and security/foreign interference: not confirmed on a page opened in session (background knowledge: Royal Assent June 22, 2023, with new Superintendent powers and a requirement for FRFI policies protecting against threats to integrity and security effective January 1, 2024). Unverified here.

- A search summary stated OSFI "will remove mandatory peer reviews starting January 2027"; I could not confirm the source page or scope (it may concern insurer actuarial peer reviews), so treat as unverified and probably not applicable to deposit-takers.

## 5. AI, crypto, quantum, fraud: what a compliance advisory manager should know

### Takeaway
OSFI has not issued a standalone AI guideline; binding AI expectations sit in E-23 (May 1, 2027), B-13 and B-10, while the OSFI-FCAC AI risk report (2024) and the FIFAI II final report (AGILE framework, listed March 23, 2026) are non-binding but signal supervisory priorities. Crypto is addressed through the capital/liquidity guideline effective Nov 1, 2026 / Jan 1, 2027 and a technology-neutral statement on tokenized deposits; no dedicated OSFI fraud guideline was found.

### Cited Findings
- OSFI and FCAC published a joint risk report, "AI Uses and Risks at Federally Regulated Financial Institutions" — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/reports-publications/osfi-fcac-risk-report-ai-uses-risks-federally-regulated-financial-institutions)
- FIFAI II final report, "AI Risks and Opportunities: Adopting an AGILE Framework in Canadian Financial Services" (listed with a March 23, 2026 date): AGILE = Awareness, Guardrails, Innovation, Learning, Ecosystem Resiliency. Built from four workshops (security and cybersecurity; financial crime; consumer protection; financial stability) run with the Department of Finance and the Global Risk Institute (search summary) — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/reports-publications/fifai-ii-ai-risks-opportunities-adopting-agile-framework-canadian-financial-services)
- FIFAI II participants identified fraud as the financial crime most likely to increase due to AI over the next two to three years; workshop 2 covered fraud management, AML, sanctions evasion and insider threats. These are workshop outputs, not binding expectations (search summary) — [Lexpert](https://www.lexpert.ca/news/finance-law/forum-on-ai-in-financial-sector-explores-importance-of-awareness-guardrails-innovation/394108)
- OSFI endorses the Financial Stability Board's sound practices for responsible AI adoption — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/risks/technology-cyber-risk-management)
- E-23 (effective May 1, 2027) applies to all models "regardless of the technology used", i.e., including AI/ML (search summary) — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-guideline-e-23-model-risk-management)
- Quantum: OSFI issued a Technology Risk Bulletin on quantum readiness in April 2024, following a December 2023 voluntary OSFI-FCAC questionnaire on AI and quantum preparedness (search summary; from the OSFI-FCAC report page) — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/reports-publications/osfi-fcac-risk-report-ai-uses-risks-federally-regulated-financial-institutions)
- Crypto: final capital and liquidity guideline effective Nov 1, 2026 / Jan 1, 2027; public disclosure of crypto-asset exposures being introduced; statement on tokenized deposits (Sept 10, 2026) — [OSFI backgrounder](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-capital-liquidity-treatment-crypto-asset-exposures-banking-guideline-2027-1); [OSFI Q2 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-strengthening-resilience-while-reducing-complexity); [OSFI Q3 2026 release](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/osfis-quarterly-release-resilience-enables-competition-innovation-sustainable-economic-growth-0)
- ARO semi-annual update (FY 2026-27): AI innovation sits in the "other risks" category and advances in AI, including autonomous capabilities, increase the effectiveness and speed of cyber-attacks (search summary) — [OSFI](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/about-osfi/reports-publications/annual-risk-outlook-semi-annual-update-fiscal-year-2026-2027)

### Inferences
- For AI governance, the compliance-relevant chain is: inventory and risk-rate AI/ML under E-23 by May 1, 2027; manage vendor AI under B-10; treat AI-driven cyber and fraud threats under B-13/E-21; and use AGILE as a benchmark OSFI may reference in supervisory dialogue.
- Fraud is addressed by OSFI indirectly (operational risk, integrity and security, AI forum output) rather than through a dedicated prudential guideline; consumer-facing fraud obligations are more likely to come from FCAC and Department of Finance measures, outside this note's scope.

### Gaps
- Publication date of the OSFI-FCAC AI report (background knowledge: September 2024) not confirmed on an opened page.
- Content of the April 2024 quantum readiness bulletin (background knowledge: it set milestones for cryptographic inventory and post-quantum migration planning) was not retrieved; no 2025–2026 quantum update was found.
- No OSFI fraud-specific guidance found; I did not search FCAC or Finance fraud measures.
- Whether OSFI has announced a dedicated AI guideline beyond E-23 as of late 2026: none found.
